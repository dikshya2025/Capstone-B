#Induustry Investigation
System 1: Kmart Australia
          Kmart was investigated to understand how a large retail organisation presents product information to customers. 
          The investigation focused on product categories, search, product names, images, prices, product descriptions and product availability.
        #Image Here#
        # Findings:
        Kmart provides a simple and organised online shopping system with clear product categories and navigation. The category system helps customers find products without 
        having to search through the entire website. The website also provides search functionality, allowing users to directly look for a specific product. Kmart displays 
        product bundles and related product options, which can help customers discover products that may be useful together. The product pages provide important information 
        such as product name, price, images, description and availability. This information allows customers to understand the product before making a purchasing decision.
        The navigation system is useful because customers can move between categories, search results and individual product pages easily. Product information is presented 
        in a way that allows customers to quickly compare products and understand their basic features.
        Overall, Kmart demonstrates the importance of organised categories, search, navigation, product details and related product information in a retail product catalogue.

System 2: JB Hi-Fi Australia
          JB Hi-Fi was investigated to understand how an electronics retailer manages detailed product information. 
          The investigation focused on product names, prices, images, brands, specifications, availability and delivery information.
          #Image Here#
          #Findings:
          JB Hi-Fi provides a product-focused online retail system with a strong emphasis on electronics and technology products. The homepage displays different product
          sections, promotions and new items, allowing customers to quickly discover products and current offers. The navigation system allows customers to browse different 
          product categories and move between product sections. Product search also helps customers locate specific products quickly. This is particularly useful because 
          JB Hi-Fi has a large number of technology products from different brands. The product detail pages provide more detailed information than a basic catalogue. 
          Customers can view the product name, price, images, product information and technical specifications. Availability and delivery or collection information are also 
          useful when deciding whether to purchase a product. The checkout process demonstrates how product information eventually connects with customer and order information. This shows that catalogue data needs to remain accurate because incorrect product prices or availability could affect later stages of the purchasing process.
          Overall, JB Hi-Fi demonstrates the importance of detailed product information, categories, search, new products, availability and checkout integration.
          
System 3: Amazon Australia
          Amazon was investigated to understand how a large online marketplace provides detailed product information. 
          The investigation focused on product names, images, prices, ratings, product features, specifications, variations and delivery information.
          #Image Here#
          #Findings: 
          Amazon provides a large and detailed online product catalogue with extensive search and navigation features. Customers can search for products using keywords and
          can narrow results using different filters. Search results can include different products across a wide price range, making it easier for customers to find products
          that match their budget.Amazon displays many products in search results and provides detailed product pages when a customer selects an item. Product pages can
          include product names, images, prices, ratings, descriptions, specifications and delivery information. Another useful feature is the display of similar or related
          products underneath or around a product page. This allows customers to discover alternative products without returning to the main search page. Amazon also uses 
          information about customer activity and preferences to provide personalised recommendations and related products. This investigation shows that a modern product 
          catalogue can do more than simply store product information. It can help customers discover products through search, filters, recommendations, similar products
          and personalised information.
          Overall, Amazon demonstrates the importance of advanced search, price filtering, detailed product information, related products and user-focused recommendations.


Data Discovery Findings
    The investigation of Kmart, JB Hi-Fi and Amazon helped us understand the amount and type of data required for a modern Product Catalogue system. The investigation 
    showed that product information is not limited to a product name, description and price. Retail systems use many different types of data to help customers find, 
    understand and compare products. Kmart demonstrated the importance of categories, navigation, search, product details, product bundles and related products. 
    JB Hi-Fi showed the importance of detailed product information, new products, product specifications, availability and the connection between product information 
    and the checkout process. Amazon demonstrated more advanced features such as search filters, different price ranges, large numbers of products, detailed product pages,
    similar item recommendations and information based on user preferences. 
      
  From these findings, our Product Catalogue system could be improved by storing additional data such as brand, SKU, product images, specifications, stock level, 
  availability, colour, size, dimensions and discount prices. Search and filtering could also be improved to help customers find products more quickly. The findings
  will be useful for future database and backend development because they provide real-world examples of the data and features that customers expect from modern retail systems.
